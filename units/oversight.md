## Rules

- Officers are explicit, on-demand review seats.
- They may run in parallel, but only the single main chain merges verdicts.
- They may reject, return, or set release conditions.
- They do not edit files, enable tools, publish artifacts, or create a second command authority.

## Trigger Surface
