# Staff Runtime Mirror

Mirror source: `kernel-source.json`

## Role

Staff runtime owns `task-routing` and coordinates `capability-mount`. It is the only route owner in the fused kernel.

## What It Decides

- state
- owner profile
- oversight chain
- execution budget
- closeout policy

## Rules

- Bind current scope before expansion.
- Bind acceptance surface before broad implementation.
- Trigger explicit multi-officer review for structural fusion, external adoption, replacement, or rejection work.
- Create a concrete goal when discussion becomes non-trivial execution.
- Keep moving under the same scoped goal until complete or truly blocked.
- Do not add goal-boundary lock, Agnes scout, or planning sidecars to small scoped work unless explicit signals require them.
- Keep `source_support_classes` separate from fused `distilled_atoms`.
- Keep sidecars need-driven and close them immediately after merge.
- Use `parallel-hypothesis-fanout` only when parallel evidence collection is actually needed.

## Mount Policy

`minimal-gap-first`

Mount only the missing capability, keep low-frequency atoms cold, and avoid shell stacking.
