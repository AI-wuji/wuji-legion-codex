# Wuji Writing Suite

Select a scenario, not a source project:

- `long-form`: structure, evidence, hooks, transitions, and natural rhythm.
- `humanize`: remove mechanical AI patterns, then score authorship and revise once.
- `translation`: preserve glossary, tone, formatting, and unresolved terms.
- `social-publish`: adapt one canonical article to the requested platform; never publish without explicit authority.

Mount only the source packages mapped to the selected scenario in `../../manifest.json`. Produce one coherent voice and one final artifact; do not concatenate outputs from multiple writers.
